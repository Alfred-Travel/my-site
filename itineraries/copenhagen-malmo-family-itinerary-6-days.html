Sample plan

A calm six-day route

A practical starting point for families who want two cities without turning every day into a transfer day.

Day 1 — Copenhagen arrival and a short first loop

01
Plan

Arrive at Copenhagen Airport; check in near Indre By or Vesterbro; keep the first walk close to the hotel and finish with an early dinner.

Day 2 — Harbour, centre, and one anchor

02
Plan

Start with Nyhavn and the harbour; choose one timed anchor such as the National Museum or Round Tower; leave a flexible late afternoon.

Day 3 — Families choose their pace

03
Plan

Use a low-transfer day around Tivoli or Frederiksberg; keep one indoor alternative ready for rain and avoid stacking a second ticketed attraction.

Day 4 — Copenhagen to Malmö

04
Plan

Take the Øresund train after breakfast; check the live timetable and passport requirements; settle in before a compact Gamla Väster and waterfront walk.

Day 5 — Malmö by neighbourhood

05
Plan

Pair Malmöhus Castle with the old town or Ribersborg rather than crossing the city repeatedly; leave time for a fika stop and an early evening.

Day 6 — Departure buffer

06
Plan

Keep the final morning close to the accommodation; confirm the airport route, departure time, and luggage plan before leaving for Copenhagen Airport or Malmö Airport.

Planning notes

Copenhagen and Malmö trip planning FAQ

Where to stay

Choose a base near a metro or major station in Copenhagen, then stay near central Malmö for the shorter second half.

When to cross

Travel after breakfast and check the live Øresund timetable, service notices, and any applicable entry requirements on the day.

What can bend

Tivoli, museums, and waterfront walks can swap order. Keep one indoor option and one low-effort option for each city.
