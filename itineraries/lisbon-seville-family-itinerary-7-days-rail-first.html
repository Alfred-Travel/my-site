Day-by-day plan

Day 1 — Arrive in Lisbon · Baixa reset

01

Route and decision Arrive, settle near Baixa or Avenida, and keep the first evening local with Praça do Comércio and an early dinner. If arrival is late, skip sightseeing and protect sleep.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 2 — Alfama and the riverfront

02

Route and decision Start in Alfama, choose one main viewpoint, then continue toward the riverfront. Use a tram or taxi when hills and heat become a constraint; do not stack multiple timed attractions.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 3 — Belém cluster and a flexible afternoon

03

Route and decision Keep Belém together: one landmark, the waterfront and a relaxed meal. Reserve the afternoon for rest or an indoor museum, depending on weather and the family’s energy.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 4 — Protected Lisbon → Seville move

04

Route and decision Pack before breakfast and verify the actual date-specific provider itinerary, connections, border requirements and luggage plan. CP’s passenger guidance is a starting point for Portugal rail information; do not assume a direct train, fare or availability. On arrival in Seville, check in near Santa Cruz or Alameda and keep the evening short.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 5 — Santa Cruz and Seville centre

05

Route and decision Walk a compact Santa Cruz route and choose either the Cathedral area or a shaded plaza as the anchor. Leave time for a long lunch and remove the optional stop first if the heat rises.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 6 — Plaza de España, María Luisa and Triana

06

Route and decision Use Plaza de España and María Luisa Park as the morning cluster, then cross to Triana only if the family still has capacity. In hot weather, shorten the walk and shift the riverfront to the cooler evening.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 7 — Buffer and departure

07

Route and decision Work backward from the confirmed departure airport, terminal and transfer. Keep only a nearby breakfast or market stop that fits the luggage and buffer; otherwise go directly to the airport.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.
