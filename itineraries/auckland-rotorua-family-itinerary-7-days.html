Preview of the full 7-day itinerary

A family route that treats the North Island transfer as an operating day instead of hiding it inside a packed sightseeing schedule.

Day 1 — Auckland arrival and soft landing

01
Route and decision

Arrive at Auckland Airport, confirm the transfer or rental-car collection, settle near a practical central or waterfront base, and keep the first evening local.

Day 2 — Auckland waterfront and one anchor

02
Route and decision

Choose the waterfront, Auckland Museum or a family-friendly ferry outing as the main anchor. Keep lunch and the afternoon nearby; use an indoor museum option if weather or jet lag wins.

Day 3 — Auckland choice day

03
Route and decision

Pick one route cluster such as the zoo and Western Springs or Devonport and the waterfront. Do not stack both. Confirm opening hours, tickets, access and the child’s energy before fixing the sequence.

Day 4 — Auckland to Rotorua: transfer first

04
Route and decision

Treat the drive as the day’s main operating task. Check NZTA traffic, road conditions, weather, rest stops, car-seat needs and accommodation check-in before adding a stop. A direct transfer is safer than a sightseeing race.

Day 5 — Rotorua geothermal anchor

05
Route and decision

Choose one confirmed geothermal or cultural anchor, then leave a recovery window. Recheck site guidance, walking surfaces, sulphur/heat considerations, age suitability and weather before departure.

Day 6 — Rotorua flexible nature day

06
Route and decision

Choose between a lakefront/forest option and a lower-energy indoor or cultural alternative. Keep the route close to the base and confirm road, operator and cancellation terms directly.

Day 7 — Departure buffer

07
Route and decision

If departing from Auckland, protect the return drive and flight buffer; if departing Rotorua, confirm the airport transfer and check-in requirements. Keep the final morning local and editable.

Planning notes

Auckland and Rotorua trip planning FAQ

Base choice

Auckland is the soft landing; Rotorua works as the compact geothermal and lake-region base. Confirm accommodation, parking and family facilities against the route.

Drive boundary

The Auckland–Rotorua move is a transfer-first day. Check current NZTA conditions, rest stops, vehicle rules and weather before adding detours.

Flexible alternatives

Keep an indoor or lower-energy option beside every outdoor-heavy day, and confirm age, access, opening and cancellation terms directly.
