Day-by-day plan

Day 1 — Arrival in Fukuoka · choose the right base

01

Route decision Start with the family’s arrival time and luggage, not an attraction list. Hakata is the practical rail-and-arrival base; Tenjin can suit a family prioritising central shopping and food. Keep the first evening around the chosen base and protect sleep.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 2 — Fukuoka city cluster · Ohori or Hakata

02

Route decision Choose one cluster: Ohori Park and the Fukuoka Castle ruins for an open-air, lower-pressure day, or Hakata/Kushida-area food and shopping when heat or rain makes a compact route better. Do not cross the city for a second anchor.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 3 — Dazaifu choice · half-day or protected rest

03

Route decision Dazaifu can be the cultural half-day if the family wants a shrine-and-approach-street experience. If the children are tired or the weather turns, keep the day in Fukuoka and use a nearby indoor or food-led alternative instead of forcing the excursion.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 4 — Fukuoka → Nagasaki · protected rail handoff

04

Route decision Treat the move as a travel day. Build the live JR Kyushu itinerary around the relay and Nishi Kyushu Shinkansen connection where applicable, reserve seats only after checking the current service, and avoid a timed Nagasaki attraction immediately after arrival.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 5 — Nagasaki · choose a hill-light route

05

Route decision Choose one main story: Peace Park and the Atomic Bomb Museum area, or Dejima and the waterfront, then keep the second block close by. Glover Garden and hillside movement can be rewarding but add steps and weather exposure; keep a lower-effort alternative visible.
