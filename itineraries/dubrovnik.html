3-day preview

A condensed look at how Alfred structures a Dubrovnik trip — arrival flow, Old Town density, and lower-friction day pacing.

Day 1 — Arrival & Old Town orientation
• Arrive via Dubrovnik Airport or Gruž port; transfer to Ploče, Old Town edge, or Lapad hotel with baggage and stair burden checked
• Lunch: simple seafood or black risotto near the walls after check-in
• Afternoon: Stradun, Rector's Palace, and a short city walls segment timed to avoid peak heat
• Evening: sunset viewpoint or harbor-side dinner with a short return route

Day 2 — Forts, island break, and crowd management
• Morning: full city walls or Lovrijenac block early before cruise-day crowding builds
• Lunch: shaded Old Town or Pile Gate area with a lower queue profile
• Afternoon: Lokrum or Banje beach depending on weather, energy, and ferry timing
• Evening: relaxed dinner in Ploče or Lapad instead of another high-friction Old Town loop

Day 3 — Flexible Croatia extension
• Morning: cable car and Srđ viewpoint or a slower harbor walk if temperatures are high
• Lunch: Gruž market area or marina-side meal before the next transfer
• Afternoon: plan onward movement to Split, Kotor, or island ferry legs with buffers checked
• Evening: final Old Town lap only if flight, ferry, and luggage timing still support it
