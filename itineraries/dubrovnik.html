Preview of the full 7-day itinerary

A condensed look at how Alfred structures a Dubrovnik trip — arrival flow, Old Town density, and lower-friction day pacing.

Day 1 — Arrival & Old Town orientation

01
Arrival

Arrive via Dubrovnik Airport or Gruž port; transfer to Ploče, Old Town edge, or Lapad hotel with baggage and stair burden checked

02
Lunch

simple seafood or black risotto near the walls after check-in

03
Afternoon

Stradun, Rector's Palace, and a short city walls segment timed to avoid peak heat

04
Evening

sunset viewpoint or harbor-side dinner with a short return route

Day 2 — Forts, island break, and crowd management

05
Morning

full city walls or Lovrijenac block early before cruise-day crowding builds

06
Lunch

shaded Old Town or Pile Gate area with a lower queue profile

07
Afternoon

Lokrum or Banje beach depending on weather, energy, and ferry timing

08
Evening

relaxed dinner in Ploče or Lapad instead of another high-friction Old Town loop

Day 3 — Flexible Croatia extension

09
Morning

cable car and Srđ viewpoint or a slower harbor walk if temperatures are high

10
Lunch

Gruž market area or marina-side meal before the next transfer

11
Afternoon

plan onward movement to Split, Kotor, or island ferry legs with buffers checked

12
Evening

final Old Town lap only if flight, ferry, and luggage timing still support it

This itinerary prioritizes Ploče, Lapad, or the Old Town edge to reduce friction between walking, views and beach time.

Local transport

Typical Dubrovnik movement in this plan uses walk, bus, and short ferry hops depending on daily sequencing.
