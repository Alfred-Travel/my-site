Preview of the full 6-day itinerary

A family route that treats the Arthur's Pass transfer as an operating day instead of hiding it inside a packed sightseeing schedule.

Day 1 — Wellington arrival and soft landing

01
Route and decision

Arrive, confirm the airport transfer or rental-car collection, settle near a practical central base and keep the first evening local.

Day 2 — Wellington indoor anchor

02
Route and decision

Make Te Papa or another confirmed central museum the main anchor. Keep meals and the afternoon nearby; preserve an outdoor option only if weather and energy cooperate.

Day 3 — Wellington choice day

03
Route and decision

Choose one compact cluster such as the waterfront and cable car or Zealandia and a nearby suburb. Do not stack both. Confirm hours, tickets, access and the child’s energy first.

Day 4 — Wellington to Arthur's Pass: transfer first

04
Route and decision

Treat the Remutaka crossing and regional drive as the day’s main operating task. Check NZTA traffic, road conditions, weather, rest stops, car-seat needs and check-in before adding a stop.

Day 5 — Arthur's Pass anchor and flexible afternoon

05
Route and decision

Choose one confirmed vineyard, farm, town or nature anchor appropriate for the family, then keep the afternoon close to base. Recheck opening times, weather, age suitability and booking terms.

Day 6 — Departure buffer

06
Route and decision

Protect the return drive and flight buffer. Keep the final morning local, confirm the route and vehicle requirements, and leave the plan editable.

Planning notes

Wellington and Arthur's Pass trip planning FAQ

Base choice

Wellington is the soft landing; Arthur's Pass works as the slower regional base. Confirm accommodation, parking and family facilities against the route.

Drive boundary

The Wellington–Arthur's Pass move is a transfer-first day. Check current NZTA conditions, rest stops, vehicle rules and weather before adding detours.

Flexible alternatives

Keep an indoor or lower-energy option beside every outdoor-heavy day, and confirm age, access, opening and cancellation terms directly.
