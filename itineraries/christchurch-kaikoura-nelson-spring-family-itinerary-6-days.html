Day-by-day plan

Day 1 — Arrive in Christchurch and reset in the gardens

01
Plan and decision

Keep the first day local. After check-in, choose Christchurch Botanic Gardens or a short central walk for 60–90 minutes, then dinner near the base. Do not add a distant attraction after arrival.

Live check: Confirm accommodation access, arrival transfer, weather and a nearby meal.

Day 2 — Christchurch spring culture and a flexible afternoon

02
Plan and decision

Morning: pair one indoor stop such as Canterbury Museum or Tūranga with a compact central route. Lunch near Riverside Market; afternoon returns to the gardens or base for recovery rather than another long transfer.

Live check: Check seasonal hours, accessibility, timed entry and an indoor fallback.

Day 3 — Transfer to Kaikōura and protect the wildlife decision

03
Plan and decision

Travel north after checking road conditions and the vehicle/transfer plan. Keep the afternoon light after arrival and use the waterfront only if energy allows. Do not schedule the marine activity on the arrival transfer.

Live check: Recheck road status, transfer duration, accommodation check-in, sea conditions and operator rescheduling terms.

Day 4 — Kaikōura wildlife anchor with shore fallback

04
Plan and decision

Use one confirmed whale, dolphin, seal or coastal experience as the 2–4 hour anchor. Keep a shore-based coastal walk or local food stop for sea/weather disruption; do not imply a reservation from a link click.

Live check: Confirm marine conditions, minimum ages, accessibility, cancellation terms and the shore fallback.

Day 5 — Transfer to Nelson with one local stop

05
Plan and decision

Protect the move west. Choose one nearby waterfront, market, garden or food stop only after checking travel time and road conditions; do not stack distant attractions onto the transfer.

Live check: Confirm the route, road conditions, Nelson check-in and the stop’s seasonal opening hours.

Day 6 — Nelson morning and departure buffer

06
Plan and decision

Choose a low-commitment local morning near the base, then preserve the onward departure. If returning to Christchurch, treat that transfer as a separate decision and do not assume same-day airport safety.

Live check: Confirm the onward route, departure margin, vehicle/transfer terms and weather before adding a final stop.
