Planning notes

Sydney family planning FAQ

One base or two?

A central Sydney base reduces hotel moves; a western base may shorten Blue Mountains access but adds city-transfer friction.

What if it rains?

Swap outdoor harbour time for a confirmed indoor museum or activity while preserving lunch and the return-time logic.

What must be confirmed?

Transport, trackwork, opening hours, weather, accessibility, fares, attraction sessions and accommodation terms.
