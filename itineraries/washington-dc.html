Day 1 — Arrival and National Mall orientation

01
Arrival

Settle near a practical Metro-connected base and keep the first evening local.

01
Pacing

Use a short monument or waterfront walk only if arrival energy allows.

Day 2 — National Mall east cluster

02
Anchor

Choose one or two confirmed museums near the Capitol/east Mall area; check timed entry, security and opening hours.

02
Fallback

Keep a nearby indoor café or shorter museum route available.

Day 3 — National Mall west cluster

03
Cluster

Group the Lincoln, Reflecting Pool and one confirmed museum/monument route geographically.

03
Heat rule

Start early, protect shade and lunch, and do not stack distant reservations.

Day 4 — Family museum choice

04
Choice

Select one main Smithsonian or family-focused indoor anchor and allow time for the group to reset.

04
Decision

Use the afternoon for a nearby neighbourhood rather than another cross-city transfer.

Day 5 — Neighbourhood and river day

05
Cluster

Choose Georgetown, Capitol Hill or another single neighbourhood/river cluster.

05
Fallback

Keep Metro-accessible indoor options visible for heat, rain or walking fatigue.

Day 6 — Flexible history or park day

06
Anchor

Choose one confirmed historic site, park or museum that fills the group’s strongest remaining interest.

06
Check

Confirm reservation, security, access and transport before fixing the day.

Day 7 — Departure buffer

07
Departure

Keep the morning near the base and protect airport or rail transfer time.

07
Boundary

Avoid a timed attraction unless departure timing and security margin are confirmed.
