Sample plan

Six days across Kyoto and Osaka

Day 1 — Arrival in Kyoto · Gion edge and an easy reset

01
Route and decision

Arrive via Kansai International Airport, Osaka or Kyoto gateway according to the confirmed booking. Keep the first evening to a short walk around Yasaka Shrine and Gion Shirakawa, with dinner near the hotel. If arrival is late, skip the walk and use a nearby meal; do not add a fixed attraction.

Day 2 — Eastern Kyoto · Kiyomizu-dera and Higashiyama

02
Route and decision

Start at Kiyomizu-dera, then walk downhill through Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka toward Yasaka Shrine. Keep Maruyama Park as the breathing-space stop. In rain or with tired children, replace the longer walk with Kyoto Railway Museum or an indoor craft activity.

Day 3 — Northern Kyoto · Arashiyama cluster

03
Route and decision

Use an early start for Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, Tenryu-ji and the Togetsukyo Bridge, keeping the cluster west of the river rather than crossing the city repeatedly. Treat the monkey park as optional because of the uphill path. If heat or fatigue rises, keep Tenryu-ji and the bridge and return to the base.

Day 4 — Protected rail move · Kyoto to Osaka

04
Route and decision

Pack before breakfast, allow time for the station and reserve seats only after checking the current JR West guidance. JR West’s Kansai WIDE page explains that pass validity, reserved seating and eligible services vary; verify the relevant ticket and train for the family’s actual dates. In Osaka, check in near Namba or Umeda and keep the evening local.

Day 5 — Osaka anchor · Osaka Castle and Dotonbori

05
Route and decision

Choose Osaka Castle grounds and museum timing as the daytime anchor, then move to Dotonbori and Hozenji Yokocho for an evening food walk. Use Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan as the rainy-day alternative; do not promise same-day availability without checking the operator.

Day 6 — Departure buffer · Osaka base to airport

06
Route and decision

Keep the morning light. Confirm the departure airport, terminal and current rail or airport-transfer plan before leaving the hotel. If the flight is late, use Kuromon Market or a nearby café only if luggage and opening hours fit; otherwise protect the airport buffer.

Planning notes

Kyoto and Osaka family planning notes

Transport source

Before using a rail pass or reserved seat, read the JR West Kansai WIDE Area Pass guidance. It lists eligible services, reservation requirements, pricing and exclusions; rules and prices can change.

Arrival and departure

Choose the first base only after checking the actual airport, arrival time, luggage plan and hotel check-in. On departure day, work backward from the flight and terminal rather than assuming a generic transfer time.

Weather, mobility and pace

For rain, keep Kyoto Railway Museum or Osaka Aquarium as alternatives. For mobility limits or young children, shorten the Higashiyama walk and treat Arashiyama’s optional uphill stop as removable.
