Planning notes

Lisbon and Seville trip planning FAQ

Why two bases?

Seven days can support two cities when the cross-border movement gets its own protected day rather than being hidden between attractions.

Where should a family stay?

Choose a central Lisbon base for the opening, then keep Seville’s second base close to the daily cluster you actually plan to use.

What must be confirmed?

Recheck transport schedules, timed entries, hotel terms, weather, and airport transfers with the relevant provider before booking.
