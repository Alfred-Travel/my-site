Planning notes

Vancouver and Victoria family planning FAQ

How should we transfer?

Compare ferry and seaplane options using current operator schedules, terminal access, luggage rules, and disruption policies.

Where should we stay?

Choose a base that makes airport, terminal, and daily walking practical; confirm property-level details rather than relying on a neighbourhood label.

What if it rains?

Keep an indoor anchor available and avoid tightly timing an outdoor activity immediately before the inter-city transfer.
