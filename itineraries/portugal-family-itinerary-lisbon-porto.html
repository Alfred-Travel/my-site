Day 1 — Lisbon arrival and local reset

01

Plan and decision Allow time for immigration, bags, airport transfer, check-in, food, and jet lag. Choose a base with practical access to the family’s likely Lisbon clusters; confirm room configuration, lift access, and slope/walking expectations. Keep the first outing local: a simple meal, essentials, and a short walk only if energy allows. Pacing rule: no fixed-time major attraction immediately after a long-haul arrival. Fallback: hotel reset and an indoor meal or nearby market.
