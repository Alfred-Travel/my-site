Day 1 — Paris arrival and reset

01

Allow time for immigration, bags, airport transfer, check-in, food, and a slower first walk. Choose a practical base near a central Metro/RER connection, balancing room needs, budget, and the family’s preferred evening radius. Keep the first outing local: a simple meal and short neighborhood walk if energy allows. Pacing rule: no fixed-time major attraction on arrival day unless arrival is early with a strong recovery margin. Rain/energy fallback: hotel reset, nearby indoor café or market, and an early night.
