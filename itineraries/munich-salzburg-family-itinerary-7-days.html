This seven-day family route uses Munich and Salzburg as two bases. The trip thesis is simple: settle in near Munich’s central rail and museum cluster, make the move to Salzburg once, then keep the final days compact and departure-aware. It suits families who want city culture, one scenic rail transfer, and breathing room rather than a checklist of distant day trips.

Planning notes

Munich and Salzburg trip planning FAQ

Arrival gateway

Munich Airport is the arrival assumption. Confirm the selected airport transfer and late-arrival practicality before locking Day 1.

Family base

Choose accommodation near a station and food options. Central convenience matters more than a postcard address when children and luggage are involved.

Transport sources

Check Deutsche Bahn for the intercity rail leg and MVV for Munich local transport. Use Salzburg Tourism for destination context, then confirm current operator details.
