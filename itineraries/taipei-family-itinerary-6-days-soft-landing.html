Planning notes

Taipei trip planning FAQ

Where to stay

Zhongshan or Ximending keeps the first days practical; compare the exact hotel location with your daily clusters.

When to move outdoors

Keep Jiufen and Shifen flexible. Rain, visibility, and transport conditions can change the best day.

What to confirm

Check venue hours, family entry rules, transport timetables, and live booking terms before committing.
