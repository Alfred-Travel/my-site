Sample six-day plan

Day 1 — Hobart arrival and waterfront reset

01
Plan

Settle near central Hobart, keep the first walk local, and avoid a second timed commitment after the flight.

Day 2 — One Hobart anchor

02
Plan

Choose one museum, market, or kunanyi experience, with a weather-safe indoor alternative and an unbooked afternoon.

Day 3 — Hobart to the east or north

03
Plan

Use the road day as a transfer with one meaningful stop, not a race between every lookout; confirm current conditions.

Day 4 — Launceston base and Tamar choice

04
Plan

Arrive with time to settle, then choose one compact Tamar Valley or city cluster according to energy and opening hours.

Day 5 — Cradle or coast decision day

05
Plan

Pick one major outing only, protect the return buffer, and keep a shorter local option if weather or roads change.

Day 6 — Departure with margin

06
Plan

Keep the final morning light, allow vehicle return and airport transfer time, and confirm provider terms before leaving.

Planning notes and FAQ

Should every day be booked? No. Keep recovery and weather space open.

Is this live availability? No. Confirm transport, attractions, accommodation, and terms with providers.
