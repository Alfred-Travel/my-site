Sample plan

Preview of the five-day route

A practical city-to-coast sequence with a protected drive day and recovery space.

Day 1 — Arrival and Melbourne base

01
Route and decision

Arrive at Melbourne Airport, transfer to a central base, and keep the first evening close to the hotel. Confirm transfer timing, check-in, dinner options and the family’s arrival energy.

Day 2 — Melbourne culture and gardens

02
Route and decision

Cluster Federation Square, the NGV and Royal Botanic Gardens rather than crossing the city repeatedly. Keep one indoor alternative if weather or energy changes.

Day 3 — Great Ocean Road transfer

03
Route and decision

Make the Great Ocean Road the protected travel day. Confirm road conditions, rest stops, child breaks and whether the selected driver can safely cover the route; do not stack a timed attraction at the end.

Day 4 — Twelve Apostles and recovery

04
Route and decision

Use the Twelve Apostles and nearby lookouts as the anchor, then keep the afternoon flexible around Port Campbell. Check access, wind, daylight and walking tolerance before committing.

Day 5 — Return and departure buffer

05
Route and decision

Return to Melbourne with a deliberate buffer, recheck the airport transfer and flight timing, and keep luggage and a nearby meal option simple.

Planning notes

Great Ocean Road family trip FAQ

Transport

Confirm whether a self-drive, private transfer or coach fits the family’s breaks, luggage and road tolerance.

Coastal anchor

Keep the Twelve Apostles as the anchor, not a promise to cover every lookout in one day.

Weather and access

Check wind, daylight, walking surfaces and current access information before setting a fixed schedule.
