Day 1 — Fukuoka soft landing

01

Day 2 — Fukuoka central family anchor

02
Morning

Cluster one food, market or park anchor near the chosen base rather than crossing the city.

02
Fallback

Day 3 — Fukuoka flexible day

03
Choice

Choose a single cultural, waterfront or family activity; check current hours, access and timed entry.

Pacing

04

Make the rail movement the day’s main task. Confirm departure time, luggage, local transfer and check-in before adding a stop.

04
Arrival

Keep the first Beppu evening close to the accommodation and avoid a timed attraction.

Day 5 — Beppu slow day

05
Anchor

Choose one confirmed hot-spring town, family-suitable attraction or food experience.

05
Fallback

06
