Seoul and Busan family trip FAQ

Where to stay

Compare lift access, room configuration, and daily walking from Myeongdong, Euljiro, Insadong, Seomyeon, or Haeundae.

When to transfer

Keep the KTX move after the first Seoul block; confirm current operator timing and station access.

What if it rains?

Use the indoor Seoul anchor or replace the coastal Busan block with a flexible market and museum day.
