Day-by-day plan

Day 1 — Arrive and reset in central Perth

01

Route and decision Stay close to the CBD or a well-connected inner suburb. Stay near Perth Station or Elizabeth Quay so the first evening is a short, low-friction walk to Elizabeth Quay, Yagan Square, or Northbridge rather than a cross-city transfer. Keep dinner close and protect sleep.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 2 — Kings Park and the river edge

02

Route and decision Start at Kings Park and Botanic Garden early, using the Fraser Avenue precinct and Lotterywest Federation Walkway as the main outdoor loop. Choose either Elizabeth Quay or the WA Museum Boola Bardip for the afternoon—not both—and remove the second viewpoint first if heat builds.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 3 — Fremantle by the simplest current connection

03

Route and decision Use Transperth’s Fremantle Line from Perth Station to Fremantle Station, checking service alerts, lifts, fares, and walking tolerance for the actual date. From the station, cluster Fremantle Markets and Cappuccino Strip/High Street first; then choose Fishing Boat Harbour for an early lunch or the WA Maritime Museum for an indoor reset. Add Fremantle Prison only if the family wants a timed, history-led visit; do not stack all three. Return on the rail line before the late-afternoon heat and fatigue window.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 4 — Wildlife, beach, or a weather-led swap

04

Route and decision Choose one Western Australian contrast day. Rottnest Island is the higher-logistics option: confirm ferry departure from Fremantle, sea conditions, bike/shuttle needs, and a hard return buffer before committing. The lower-risk alternative is a Fremantle extension—WA Maritime Museum plus Fishing Boat Harbour—or Cottesloe Beach from Fremantle, with shade and an early return. Do not combine Rottnest with Kings Park or a late airport day.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 5 — Buffer, breakfast, and departure

05

Route and decision For Perth Airport, work backward from the confirmed terminal and the Transperth Airport Line or pre-booked transfer. Keep luggage staged before breakfast; if the flight is later, use one short Elizabeth Quay or Northbridge stop only. Do not add Fremantle on departure day unless the airport transfer, luggage storage, and terminal buffer are already confirmed.
