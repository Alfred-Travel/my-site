Preview of the full 7-day itinerary

A condensed look at how Alfred structures a Christchurch trip—arrival gateway, neighborhoods, and daily pacing.

Day 1 — Arrival, Central City Reset & Riverside Logic

01
Arrival

Arrive at CHC; taxi, shuttle, or rideshare to the central city/Riccarton base (validated 20–30 min)

02
Check-in

Check-in near the central city, Riverside, or Riccarton so jet-lag recovery stays walkable; easy lunch at Riverside Market

03
Afternoon

Avon River / Botanic Gardens / Tram corridor loop instead of stacking long transfers on arrival day

04
Evening

early dinner in the central city; keep the first night light if the trip includes children or a same-day Australian arrival

Day 2 — Family-Friendly Christchurch Without Cross-City Friction

05
Morning

Margaret Mahy Playground, Tūranga, and nearby central-city blocks while energy is highest

06
Lunch

casual central-city stop before moving east or south

07
Afternoon

choose one clean second block — Christchurch Gondola + Sumner finish OR Willowbank / Orana style wildlife stop, depending weather and family pace

08
Evening

dinner back near the hotel so the day does not end with a heavy return leg across town

Day 3 — Scenic Extension or Easy Buffer Day

09
Morning

half-day to Akaroa OR a slower local day with markets, museums, and riverfront time depending driving tolerance

10
Lunch

Akaroa harbour meal or a central-city café if keeping the day local

11
Afternoon

souvenir, snack, and repack buffer before departure rather than over-programming the final block

12
Evening

confirm CHC airport timing with enough margin for rental-car return or family packing delays
