Day 1 — Tokyo arrival and reset

01

Allow time for immigration, bags, airport transfer, check-in, food, and jet lag. Choose a base with practical rail access and a realistic evening radius; room configuration and lift access matter for families. Keep the first outing local: convenience-store basics, a simple meal, and a short walk if energy allows. Pacing rule: no fixed-time major attraction immediately after a long-haul arrival. Fallback: hotel reset and a nearby indoor meal or market.
