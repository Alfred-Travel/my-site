Planning notes

Questions to validate

Late arrival

What changes if arrival is late? Keep the first evening local and protect the transfer buffer.

One thing to drop

Which reservation can be dropped without breaking the route? Keep one anchor, not a full stack.

Luggage and rail

Is the Kyoto–Osaka transfer still sensible with luggage? Confirm live rail timing and station access.
