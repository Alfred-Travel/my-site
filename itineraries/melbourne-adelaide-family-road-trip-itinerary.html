Day-by-day plan

Day 1 — Melbourne arrival and neighbourhood reset

01

Arrive in Melbourne, collect luggage, and transfer to a central base such as Southbank, the CBD edge, Carlton, or an area that reduces the family’s first-day transport load. Keep the day deliberately light: settle in, walk one nearby precinct, and buy practical supplies. Why this works: It protects against flight delay and avoids spending the first evening crossing the city. If arrival is early and energy is good, add one short museum or riverside activity; do not pre-book a long cross-city sequence.

Day 2 — Melbourne’s compact family day

02

Choose one central cluster rather than trying to cover the whole city. A museum-and-gardens combination can work well, with an easy lunch and a low-commitment afternoon. Keep a weather fallback close to the same base. Pacing rule: One anchor activity plus one flexible neighbourhood block. This creates a usable first full day instead of a checklist.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 3 — Melbourne to Apollo Bay or a nearby Great Ocean Road base

03

Collect the rental car and leave Melbourne with a realistic departure window. Break the coastal drive with short, weather-dependent stops rather than promising every lookout. Overnight around Apollo Bay or another practical coastal base, depending on the family’s energy and the accommodation inventory. Transfer logic: The route is more enjoyable when the drive is treated as part of the day, not as dead time between attractions. Confirm parking, road conditions, and child-seat requirements.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 4 — Great Ocean Road westbound

04

Continue west through the major coastal viewpoints, selecting only the stops that fit the weather and daylight. Keep the walking distances short and avoid making every viewpoint mandatory. Overnight near Port Campbell or another base that protects the next day’s route. Fallback: If wind or rain makes the coast unpleasant, shorten the lookout sequence and preserve the family’s energy for a warm meal and an early night.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 5 — Port Campbell and inland transition

05

Use the morning for one or two signature coastal stops, then move inland toward the Grampians / Halls Gap area. This is a deliberate transition day, not a second full sightseeing day. Execution note: Check the actual route and road conditions before departure. A longer transfer with children needs meal and rest breaks planned in advance.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 6 — Grampians nature day with an easy option

06

Choose a short, family-appropriate walk and one viewpoint or cultural experience. Keep a lower-effort alternative available if weather, heat, or fatigue changes the plan. Overnight a second night in the same base if the accommodation and route make that practical. Trust boundary: National park access, track conditions, wildlife encounters, and seasonal closures need date-specific confirmation.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 7 — Grampians to South Australian border region / Naracoorte area

07

Travel west with one planned break rather than multiple ambitious detours. A regional overnight can make the final Adelaide drive more manageable and gives the family a reset between the nature section and city section. Route choice: If the family prefers fewer hotel changes, make this a longer transfer day to Adelaide; if they value calmer pacing, keep the regional stop and accept the extra night.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 8 — Arrive Adelaide and reset

08

Return the car according to the rental agreement and settle into a central Adelaide base. Keep the first afternoon simple: Central Market or a nearby walk if open and convenient, otherwise a hotel reset and an early dinner. Why this works: It separates the end of the road trip from the final city experience and reduces the risk that a late return or vehicle issue ruins the departure plan.

Confirm before booking: Check current schedules, access, weather, opening hours and supplier terms for this day.

Day 9 — Adelaide family morning and departure buffer

09

Choose one nearby morning activity that fits the departure time. Leave a generous airport or onward-transfer buffer and confirm luggage, vehicle return, and terminal details before leaving the hotel. Booking boundary: Do not schedule a distant attraction on departure day unless the transport plan and opening times have been checked for the exact date.
