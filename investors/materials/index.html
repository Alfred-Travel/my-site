The problem · travel remains personal

The internet made travel searchable. It didn’t make it simple.

OTAs made booking self-serve, but the hardest part of travel is still understanding the person, coordinating the whole trip, and being there when plans change.

Self-serve reality

15+ hours of research over 2 months

Families compare flights, hotels, activities, restaurants, policies, locations, and trade-offs across a fragmented search journey.

The hidden work

One trip, multiple avenues

Why concierges endure

Personalisation plus service

A trusted assistant understands the client’s needs, makes the necessary bookings, and provides the human support required when things go wrong.

Alfred brings the concierge layer to the self-serve era.
