Validation layer

Why Alfred feels different

Most planners generate text. Alfred's Logistical Validation Engine applies trip logic, transport constraints, hotel proximity, activity sequencing, and route consistency to produce plans you can actually execute.

No country lock

Plan multi-country trips in one itinerary with flights, rails, and hotels connected—no artificial single-country limit.

Turn-by-turn road logic

Route optimization and stop sequencing with practical drive windows—built for real travel days, not generic lists.

Multi-city validation

Cross-city routing with transfer sanity checks and cleaner day-to-day flow from day one to day ten.
