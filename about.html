About Alfred

The team building trusted travel planning.

Alfred exists to close the gap between travel inspiration and a trip people can actually plan, edit, and take toward booking with confidence.

Our mission

Give travellers their time back.

Travel planning moved from traditional agents to fragmented tabs, chats, and spreadsheets. Alfred is built for the next step: structured plans that stay useful as the trip takes shape.

Why Alfred exists

We believe good travel planning should feel clear, not chaotic. Alfred brings the people travelling, the destinations, the pace, and the practical decisions into one place.

The result is not just a list of ideas. It is a trip you can understand, adjust, share, and take toward booking.

The product promise

  • Start with the traveller — plan around the people and preferences that matter.
  • Keep the plan visible — make route, timing, and itinerary decisions easier to review.
  • Move toward action — take a considered plan toward booking when it feels right.
