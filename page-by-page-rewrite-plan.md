# Website V2 — Page-by-Page Rewrite and UI/UX Plan

## 1. Homepage

### Objective
Make the homepage the clearest and calmest entry into Alfred. It should feel like the public-facing version of the webapp.

### Current issues

- Hero copy is functional but still sounds like generic AI travel marketing.
- “Start your journey” and “Discover your next adventure” are too template-like.
- The page introduces technical concepts before the traveller benefit is fully clear.
- Product proof and support should be more visible.

### Recommended visual/UI direction

- Use the webapp’s dark premium visual system.
- Use the same surface, card, border, radius, and accent rules as the webapp.
- Add one strong product preview showing an itinerary or planning state.
- Use calm spacing and one clear idea per section.
- Keep one primary CTA and one secondary CTA throughout the page.

### Recommended content

#### Hero headline
> Travel planning that feels calm, not chaotic.

Alternative:

> Structured trips, ready to book.

#### Hero supporting copy
> Alfred turns messy trip planning into a clear, editable itinerary for families and multi-city travellers.

#### Primary CTA
> See how Alfred works

#### Secondary CTA
> Explore trip examples

#### Recommended sections

1. Hero
2. What Alfred does
3. How Alfred works
4. Why travellers trust Alfred
5. Trip examples
6. Support and FAQ reassurance
7. App CTA
8. Consistent footer

### Remove or de-emphasise

- “Start your journey”
- “Discover your next adventure”
- generic adventure language
- model names in the hero
- blockchain/reward references on primary marketing surfaces
- technical claims that are not immediately useful to a traveller

## 2. About / Company

### Objective
Make Alfred feel like a credible, traveller-first company rather than a technical project.

### Recommended visual/UI direction

- Match the webapp’s dark surfaces and typography.
- Use real founder/team imagery with consistent framing.
- Replace large blocks of company copy with clear narrative sections.
- Keep press coverage visually secondary to mission and product trust.

### Recommended hero copy
> The team building trusted travel planning.

### Recommended supporting copy
> Alfred exists to close the gap between travel inspiration and a trip people can actually plan, edit, and book with confidence.

### Mission rewrite direction
Explain that:

- travel planning moved from traditional agents to fragmented tabs and spreadsheets
- travellers need more than chat suggestions
- Alfred creates structured, editable plans that can move toward booking

### Recommended mission copy
> Alfred was built for travellers who want the freedom of planning without the uncertainty of piecing everything together themselves. We turn ideas into structured itineraries that can be checked, edited, and taken toward booking.

### Press section
Keep the press section, but rewrite descriptions around:

- product usefulness
- planning clarity
- traveller outcomes

Avoid leading with:

- crypto rewards
- disruption language
- overly technical product claims

### Team section
Keep the founders and credentials, but shorten descriptions and tie each person back to the traveller problem Alfred is solving.

## 3. Products / Features

### Objective
Explain the product through traveller outcomes, using the webapp interface as the visual proof.

### Recommended hero
> Everything you need to turn a trip idea into a workable plan.

### Recommended feature structure

1. Create a traveller or family profile
2. Build a trip around real constraints
3. Review and edit the itinerary
4. Move from plan to booking
5. Share the trip with the people travelling

### Recommended feature copy

#### Create a profile
> Tell Alfred who is travelling, what matters to them, and what the trip needs to accommodate.

#### Build a trip
> Start with a destination, route, dates, or idea. Alfred turns the brief into a structured plan.

#### Review and edit
> Adjust the itinerary without losing the rest of the trip. Keep the plan useful as the details change.

#### Book with confidence
> Move from planning to partner booking once the route, timing, and stay choices make sense.

#### Share the plan
> Keep the people travelling together around the same itinerary.

### Visual recommendation
Use real webapp screenshots or close product UI crops rather than generic feature illustrations.

## 10. FAQ

### Objective
Make the FAQ one of the strongest trust pages.

### Recommended category structure

- How Alfred works
- Planning and itineraries
- Booking
- Family and group travel
- Mobile access
- Privacy and data
- Support

### Recommended answers

#### How does Alfred work?
> Tell Alfred who is travelling, where you want to go, and what matters to you. Alfred turns that brief into a structured itinerary you can review, edit, and move toward booking.

#### Can I plan trips for my family?
> Yes. Add the people travelling, their preferences, and any practical needs. Alfred uses that context to create a plan that works for the group rather than one generic traveller.

#### Can Alfred book flights and hotels?
> Alfred helps move a validated plan toward partner booking when you are ready. Booking availability and terms are shown through the relevant booking partner.

#### How can I get support?
> Visit the Support Center or browse Help & FAQ. If you need account assistance, follow the support instructions for your request.

### Remove or review

- blockchain-token FAQ content unless the feature is active and strategically important
- vague “AI continuously learns” claims
- unsupported security superlatives such as “highest security standards”
- references that send users back to social links for normal support

## 14. Responsive and mobile consistency

### Objective
Make the public website feel like the webapp on smaller screens.

### Recommendations

- use the same spacing rhythm as the webapp
- keep tap targets large and obvious
- make the primary CTA persistent where appropriate
- avoid large desktop-only comparison tables without a mobile alternative
- keep itinerary cards readable and scannable
- ensure support remains visible on mobile
- use the same card and button behavior as the webapp

---

## 15. SEO and metadata content

### Objective
Keep SEO useful without allowing it to distort the product experience.

### Recommendations

- write titles for travellers first, then search intent
- avoid repeating “AI travel planner” in every title and heading
- align meta descriptions with the actual page benefit
- keep canonical URLs consistent
- retain structured data where accurate
- remove or rewrite schema claims that are outdated or too broad
- ensure article titles and excerpts are not repetitive

### Example homepage metadata

**Title:**
> Alfred Travel | Calm, booking-ready trip planning

**Description:**
> Alfred helps families and multi-city travellers turn trip ideas into clear, editable itineraries they can refine and take toward booking.
