2. MindTrip – iOS-Only, No Profile, Chat-Focused

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5)
Overall Score: 6.5/10

Best For: iOS users who want structured itineraries with booking integration.

Mobile-first only on iOS (no Android parity). AI personalization has no profile support—cannot tailor for families or multiple travellers. Features are chat-based rather than a full planning engine. Good structure and integration, but limited by platform and personalization.

3. Layla – Chat-Focused, No Mobile-First or Structure

Rating: ⭐⭐ (2/5)
Overall Score: 4.5/10

Best For: Chat-based inspiration only.

No mobile-first design, no structured day-to-day plan, no booking integration. AI personalization lacks profile support. Chat-only features—useful for ideas but not for planning or execution.

4. GuideGeek – Chat-Focused, No Mobile or Integration

Rating: ⭐⭐ (2/5)
Overall Score: 4/10

Best For: Casual chat about destinations.

Similar to Layla: desktop-centric, no structure, no integration to book. No profile-based personalization. Content is there, but you can't turn it into a bookable, structured trip.

5. TriPandoo – Planning-Only, Same-Country Limitation

Rating: ⭐ (1/5)
Overall Score: 3/10

Best For: Single-country trips only—not suitable for most travellers who do multi-country or cross-border itineraries.

TriPandoo only does same-country itineraries, which is useless for most travellers who combine cities or countries (e.g. Paris + Rome, London + Dublin, SEA multi-city). No profile-based AI personalization. No multi-trip features (A–B, multi-city, road trips) and no booking integration. Full offline access and structure don't offset these gaps.

Pricing: Free (5 days/month), Pro ($9.99/month), Premium ($24.99/month).

6. Wonderlog – Worst Overall Fit

Rating: ⭐ (1/5)
Overall Score: 3.5/10

Best For: Basic list-making only.

Mobile-first and structured lists, but no AI personalization, no multi-trip features (A–B, multi-city, road trips), no booking integration, and no depth of content. Fails four of six traveller expectations.
