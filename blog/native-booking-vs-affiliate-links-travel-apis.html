Partner Booking vs. Affiliate Links: How Alfred Hands Your Plan to Checkout

Alfred doesn’t process payments. From your saved itinerary, the Book hub opens partner checkout, such as Holiday Heroes for flight and hotel packages or Airalo for eSIMs, and the partner handles payment and booking support.

Key takeaways

  • Alfred doesn’t process payments or manage bookings. You book with the partner.
  • The Book hub sits on your itinerary, so flights and hotels, activities, eSIMs and travel insurance are all reached from the same trip.
  • Trip.com and Booking.com appear only in price compare (for beta testers), as a check against a Holiday Heroes package.

When an AI planner “suggests” a hotel or flight and then sends you to a third-party site with a referral code, the suggestion has no link back to your plan. Alfred starts from a saved, editable itinerary. When you’re ready, open Book (Book all on the last day) and choose what to book. The partner then handles payment, confirmation, changes, cancellations and booking support under its own terms.

What the Book hub covers

Tab What you do
Flights & hotels Book it on a flight or hotel, or Book Now on a Holiday Heroes package
Activities Filter by day, then Book it
eSIMs See the best-value Airalo plan for your destination, with 10% off using ALFRED10
Travel insurance Compare cover on an external site

A Holiday Heroes package checkout opens in the current tab. Flight, hotel, activity and eSIM links open in a new tab, so your itinerary stays open while you book.

What Alfred doesn’t do

  • Payments — Alfred doesn’t take payment or issue booking confirmations. The partner does.
  • Price compare — Beta testers can compare a Holiday Heroes package with Trip.com and Booking.com prices. You don’t book Trip.com or Booking.com through Alfred.
  • Multi-city swaps — On a multi-city itinerary, flights and hotels can’t be swapped once the plan is built.

Plan the trip in Alfred, check it day by day, then book with the partner from the same plan.
