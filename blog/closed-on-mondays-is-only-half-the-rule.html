Closed on Mondays is only half the rule

A generated day can put a museum on the right weekday and still send a family to a locked door, because closing days move with public holidays, temporary changes and the season.

Plan a trip Browse the blog

2026-10-07 · Alfred Team · Travel notes

“Closed on Mondays” tells you the usual day. It does not tell you which day the place is closed in your week. For that you need the venue’s own closed-days list and the country’s holiday calendar, side by side.

The signal: one museum’s October has two closed Tuesdays

The 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art, Kanazawa, says its exhibition zone closes on Mondays, and on the following weekday when a Monday is a national holiday. Its closed-days list, opened on 7 October 2026, shows four dates in October 2026: Monday 5, Tuesday 13, Monday 19 and Tuesday 27.

Japan’s Cabinet Office lists Sports Day as the second Monday in October, which is 12 October in 2026. So the museum is open that Monday and closed the Tuesday after it. The 27th is different. The same list shows a temporary opening on Monday 26 October and a temporary closure on Tuesday 27 October. No holiday explains that swap. Only the list shows it.

What breaks when the plan only knows the weekday

A family that moved the museum to a Tuesday to dodge “closed on Mondays” would have reached a closed exhibition zone on the 13th or the 27th. The same city shows how many shapes the rule takes. Each of these comes from an official page opened on 7 October 2026:

Same rule, same shift: the D.T. Suzuki Museum closes on Mondays, or the next day if the Monday is a holiday, on Visit Kanazawa, the city’s official tourism site.

Different day, opposite shift: the Kanazawa Umimirai Library closes on Wednesdays, but opens on national holidays.

Open every day, different hours: Kenroku-en opens 7:00 to 18:00 until 15 October, then 8:00 to 17:00 from 16 October. A trip that crosses that date loses an hour of morning.

Open, but not all of it: a notice on the 21st Century Museum’s site says its Swimming Pool closes from 19 to 30 October 2026. Another says the whole museum will close for renovation from 6 May 2027 to March 2028, as planned.

Open, but not to everyone: Myoryuji, the “Ninja Temple”, takes reservations by phone only, and preschoolers cannot enter. For a family with a four-year-old, that door is closed on every day of the week.

Use the closing-day test before the day goes on the plan

Write these four lines next to every anchor on the plan, from the venue’s own page. If a line is blank, the day is still a suggestion.

Four lines each anchor owes you

The weekly closing day: the usual day off, in the venue’s words, not a summary.

The holiday rule: whether a public holiday in your dates moves the closure, and which way. Check the country’s official holiday list for those dates.

The exceptions for your dates: the closed-days list, any temporary opening or closure, and anything inside the venue that is shut while you are there.

The hours on that date: whether a seasonal change falls inside your trip, and who is allowed in, such as an age limit or a booking that can only be made by phone.

Then pair each fragile anchor with one that opens every day. In Kanazawa, Kenroku-en and the Shima teahouse list no weekly closing day, so a closed door becomes a swap, not a lost day. The test is our recommendation, built from the venues’ own rules. It is not a rule any venue publishes. Lists change, so check them again the week before you go.

Where Alfred fits

Alfred starts from a travelling profile. On web Lets Go, Holiday takes an origin, a destination, dates, a budget step and a day pace, then Plan my trip queues generation. Alfred does not check a venue’s closing calendar while it writes the day, so the four lines are yours to fill in.

When the itinerary is ACTIVE, it can be viewed, edited and shared. An item’s title, notes and times can be edited by hand, so the closing day and the holiday rule can sit on the card itself. If you find a closure, tell Hey Alfred, for example “the museum is closed on Tuesday”. It answers with numbered options built from items already in your plan, such as swapping in an indoor item or rescheduling, and changes nothing until you pick one and confirm. It can also swap two full days after you confirm. It will not move a day that has a flight. Once a flight and a hotel are both confirmed, the web toolbar shows Download The App in place of Hey Alfred, and the edit form is still there.

Book opens a partner checkout. It does not hold a museum ticket or make a phone booking for you.

Practical next step

Take the anchors on your generated plan and fill in the four lines for each. Where a holiday or a temporary change moves a closure into your dates, swap that day with one built around an anchor that opens every day, and write the reason on the card so everyone in the group can see why the day moved.
