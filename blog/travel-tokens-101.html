Travel Tokens 101: How Alfred Rewards You in the App

Alfred Tokens are earned in the Alfred mobile app’s Rewards tab: claim journey and quest rewards, and solve the daily city puzzle.

Plan a tripBack to Alfred

Home / Travel Note

2026-02-03 · Alfred Team · AIO Insights

Key takeaways

  • Alfred Tokens ($ALFRD) are earned in the Alfred mobile app’s Rewards tab.
  • You earn by claiming rewards from your rewards journey and quests, and by solving the daily city puzzle.
  • Rewards aren’t on the website yet; the web token page is marked “coming soon”.

Travel 3.0 isn’t just “AI plans it”—it’s “you get something back.” Alfred Tokens are how we do that. In the Alfred mobile app, the Rewards tab shows what you can do next, what’s in progress and what you’ve completed. A red dot appears when the daily puzzle is ready or a reward is waiting to be claimed.

How you earn Alfred Tokens

Where What happens
Rewards journey Steps take you to the itinerary builder, your itineraries, family setup or the Home social links. Completed steps can become claimable rewards.
Claim Tap Claim X $ALFRD and the tokens are credited.
Quests Quests show up through the rewards journey and can be claimed when complete.
Daily city puzzle Guess the city from a landmark photo within three attempts to earn tokens and build a streak.

Your balance and transaction history sit in the Rewards tab, with a leaderboard behind the crown icon.

What to know

  • Mobile only for now: the website has no rewards screen yet.
  • Booking isn’t rewarded: Alfred doesn’t process bookings. Partners such as Holiday Heroes and Airalo handle payment and booking.
