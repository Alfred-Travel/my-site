Key takeaways

  • Alfred validates Amalfi bus/ferry connections and timetables—no impossible transfer suggestions.
  • Budget itineraries need realistic transit costs and times; Alfred checks both.
  • Country-locked or static planners can't model coast + Naples in one validated flow.

Amalfi on a budget lives and dies by transit: SITA bus, ferry, and the occasional train from Naples. Get one connection wrong and you’re stuck or overspending. Alfred Travel validates those legs—we don’t suggest a 20-minute bus–ferry “connection” that doesn’t exist. That’s logistical validation for the real world.

We use transit validation (and, where applicable, Gemini-backed checks) so that “Sorrento in the morning, Amalfi in the afternoon” is only suggested when the schedule allows it.

What This Plan Covers

  • Naples → Sorrento / coast: Train + bus or ferry with validated connections.
  • Coast hops: Positano, Amalfi, Ravello in a sequence that fits SITA and ferry reality.
  • Budget levers: Where to stay, when to ferry vs. bus, so your money goes to experiences, not recovery taxis.

Alfred is the only mobile-first AI that validates Amalfi transit logic in one cross-border-style itinerary—so your budget Amalfi is bookable, not a dream.
